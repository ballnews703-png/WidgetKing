👑 WidgetKing Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

WidgetKing is built so that your stuff stays yours. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no server of ours that receives your data. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, when, and where it goes.

The short version: your designs live on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use a feature that plainly needs it — AI design (to Anthropic) or live weather (to Open-Meteo) — and even then, only the minimum required, sent directly from your device.

Your designs

Widget designs, app-icon packs, photos you add, and settings are stored locally — in your browser's storage for the web designer, and on your phone inside the Scriptable app for installed widgets. We never receive or store a copy. Backups you export are files you keep; they are never uploaded.

AI design (optional)

The AI designer only works if you set it up with an access key. When you use it:

  • Your widget description — and, if you use "Clone a widget" or the design-review feature, an image of the widget being designed — is sent directly from your device to Anthropic (the AI provider) to generate the design. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Your access key is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us (we have no server), and it is not included in exported backup files.

If you never set up the AI, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Weather widgets (optional)

Widgets that show live weather ask your phone for your approximate location (accurate to about a kilometer) and send only those coordinates to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to fetch the forecast. See Open-Meteo's terms & privacy. The location is kept on your device and results are cached there. Widgets without weather never request your location.

Sync and share links

When you create a sync link or share a design, the designs themselves are encoded inside the link — they travel in the link, not through a server. A sync link can optionally include your AI access key so your other device is ready to go. Treat sync links like passwords: only send them to yourself or someone you trust, because anyone who has the link has its contents. Opening a link always shows a summary and asks for confirmation before anything is added to a device.

Widget updates

The installed widget script can check this website for a newer version of itself. That check is a plain download — it sends no personal data, and your designs never leave the device during an update.

Children

WidgetKing does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — it has no mechanism to collect personal information at all.

Changes

If WidgetKing ever adds features that change what leaves your device (for example, optional accounts for syncing), this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the WidgetKing GitHub repository.
