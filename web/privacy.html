WidgetKing Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

WidgetKing is built so that your stuff stays yours. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no account unless you choose to create one. This page explains exactly what leaves your device, when, and where it goes.

The short version: your designs live on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use a feature that plainly needs it — AI design (to Anthropic), live data such as weather (to the service that provides it), or the optional account that syncs your designs between your own devices — and even then, only the minimum required, sent directly from your device.

Your designs

Widget designs, app-icon packs, photos you add, and settings are stored locally — in your browser's storage for the web designer, and on your phone inside the Scriptable app for installed widgets. Unless you sign in to sync (below), we never receive or store a copy. Backups you export are files you keep; they are never uploaded.

Account & sync (optional)

You can create an account so your designs follow you between your own devices. If you do:

  • What we collect: your email address, used only to send you a 6-digit sign-in code. There are no passwords.
  • What is stored: your widget designs (their layout, text, and settings). Photos you place in widgets are not uploaded — they stay on the device that holds them. Your AI access key is never uploaded.
  • Where: in a database hosted by Supabase, our infrastructure provider, in the United States. Each account can only ever read and write its own rows — the database enforces this, not just the app.
  • Deleting: signing out keeps everything on your device and stops syncing. To delete your account and every design stored with it, use the delete option in Settings → Account, or contact us (below); the data is removed within 30 days.

If you never sign in, nothing about this section applies — no email is collected and no design leaves your device.

AI design (optional)

The AI designer only works if you set it up with an access key. When you use it:

  • Your widget description — and, if you use "Clone a widget" or the design-review feature, an image of the widget being designed — is sent directly from your device to Anthropic (the AI provider) to generate the design. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Your access key is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, never synced to your account, and not included in exported backup files.

If you never set up the AI, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Live data in widgets (optional)

Some widget pieces fetch live information. Each request goes directly from your phone to the service named, with only what that service needs:

  • Weather and sun & moon — your approximate location (accurate to about a kilometer) is sent to Open-Meteo. The location is kept on your device and results are cached there. Widgets without these pieces never request your location.
  • Stocks — the ticker symbols you chose are sent to Yahoo Finance.
  • News — a public headline feed is downloaded from Google News; nothing about you is sent.
  • Fantasy sports — the Sleeper username or league you entered is sent to Sleeper's public API.
  • App icons — app names you search for are sent to Apple's App Store search to find their icons.
  • Stickers and updates — artwork from WidgetKing's sticker library and the widget script's update check are plain downloads from this website. They carry no personal data; like any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) sees the request itself.

Calendar, reminders, and battery pieces read that information on your phone only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Share links and device-to-device links

When you share a design or send all your designs to another device as a link, the designs themselves are encoded inside the link — they travel in the link, not through a server. A device-to-device link can optionally include your AI access key so the other device is ready to go. Treat such links like passwords: only send them to yourself or someone you trust, because anyone who has the link has its contents. Opening a link always shows a summary and asks for confirmation before anything is added to a device.

Children

WidgetKing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only personal information the app can collect is an email address, and only when someone chooses to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When WidgetKing adds or changes anything that affects what leaves your device, this policy is updated in the same release and the date at the top changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a deletion request? Open an issue on the WidgetKing GitHub repository.
